Source: DFO Fantasy Show, center preview episode (2026-27 season), hosts Brock Zajac and Bibbs Bondi. Transcript is raw auto-captions (no punctuation, some names garbled by the ASR) — treat player names below as the corrected versions.

**Two different ADP numbers show up throughout, don't conflate them:**
- *Overall ADP* — average draft position across all positions, straight off the draft platform.
- *Position ADP rank* — where that overall ADP places him among centers only (e.g. "12th center off the board"). The gap between a player's consensus rank (1-10 below) and his position ADP rank is the show's stated edge/reach.
- *True ADP* — Brock's own adjustment for players who go undrafted in most leagues: it factors in draft rate (% of leagues a player is drafted in at all), so a guy taken 146th on average but only drafted in 12% of leagues gets a true ADP far later than 146. Only used for the deep-sleeper picks below, not the top 10.
